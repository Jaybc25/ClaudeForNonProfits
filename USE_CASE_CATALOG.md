# Nonprofit catalog and filter map

36 illustrative workflows; 12 featured pilots; 8 mission areas; 7 organization forms; 11 functions.

## Browsing model

Mission area and organization form are separate facets. An annual expense band offers implementation guidance without implying that smaller organizations cannot use a workflow. These are editorial browsing categories, not legal classifications, product eligibility or adoption evidence.

Mission areas: Human services; Education & research; Health & mental health; Arts, culture & humanities; Environment & animals; International development & relief; Public & societal benefit; Religion & faith-based service.

Forms: Direct-service charity; Foundation & grantmaker; Advocacy & social welfare; Membership & trade association; Faith-based congregation; Fiscally sponsored project; Coalition & intermediary.

Annual expense bands: under $500K; $500K to under $10M; $10M and above. Bands are planning conveniences, not readiness scores. Staff, data quality, technical ownership and operating support matter independently of budget.

## Evidence and limits

All workflow summaries, route assignments, pilots and metrics are JayAI hypotheses. The featured set is an editorial shortlist of bounded, reviewed, measurable work, not a ranking based on outcomes. The catalog does not estimate grant win rate, donor retention, eligibility or clinical performance. No survey percentages are presented as current benchmarks.

Foundation workflows can span any mission. Faith-based organizations can work in several mission areas. Fiscal sponsorship does not by itself establish eligibility; verify the sponsor and project with the vendor. Hospitals and universities are not operational focus areas in this catalog.

## Program versus catalog

Browsing a workflow is not confirmation of a program discount. Team’s standard nonprofit list rate is $8 per user/month with a 2-seat minimum; the official program page also lists $3 for eligible nonprofits in low- or middle-income countries. Enterprise requires a quote here because official sources differ. No nonprofit discount is applied to API token rates. Confirm Code, task features, usage limits, connector access and any separate vendor costs for the exact purchased plan.
