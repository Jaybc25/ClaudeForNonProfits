03 / Make the case

A useful estimate shows its work.

Cost alone cannot tell you whether a service is better. Build a pilot case around workload volume, full staff effort, operating cost, capacity, service measures, and evidence from a bounded test.

Build a pilot value case ↗
  1. 01
    Define the baseline

    How is the work done today, and what does success mean for participants and staff?

  2. 02
    Model cost transparently

    Use current, dated inputs and the right cost method for each Claude route.

  3. 03
    Test the value

    Measure quality, time returned, adoption, and service outcomes before scaling.
