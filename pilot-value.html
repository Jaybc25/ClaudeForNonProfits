How it is calculated

Time difference × monthly volume × 12 × realization factor = annual capacity hours. Hours × loaded hourly cost = annual capacity value proxy. Three-year net = three years of that value minus three years of recurring cost and one-time implementation.

Planning interpretation

Time returned is capacity for higher-value work, better service, or backlog reduction. It is not a payroll reduction or a cash saving. “Capacity ROI” divides modeled three-year net capacity value by modeled three-year cost. A positive ratio does not establish that service quality, equity, safety, or eligibility controls are acceptable. Volunteer hours are mission capacity, not payroll savings. Enter $0 for the hourly value if you want an hours-only case; record any chosen volunteer-hour proxy separately. The example inputs are invented; replace them with observed baseline and pilot data. Calculations run in your browser; the API cost handoff places the estimate in the URL.
