How this catalog was made

The featured set is an editorial shortlist of bounded, measurable pilot ideas. All workflows are illustrative JayAI proposals, not verified adoption or outcome claims. Mission areas are practical browsing categories informed by NCCS’s NTEE taxonomy ↗; missions overlap, so one workflow can fit several types. Organization form, function, and Claude route tags are starting hypotheses to test in discovery.

Sector themes were checked against Anthropic’s nonprofit resources ↗ on September 30, 2026. A catalog match does not establish eligibility for Anthropic’s nonprofit program. No measured benefits from other organizations are used as pilot assumptions.
